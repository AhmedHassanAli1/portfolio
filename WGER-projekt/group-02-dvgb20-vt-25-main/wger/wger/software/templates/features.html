Do I need an account to use Kaufit?

No, you can explore most features without logging in, but saving your progress requires an account.

Can I use Kaufit on my phone?

Yes, the platform is mobile-friendly and works in your browser without needing to install an app.

Is my data secure?

Yes, Kaufit does not collect or sell personal data. Everything stays private on your account.
